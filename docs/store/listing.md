## Description

SmartHomeKJ puts every light, plug, thermostat, lock, camera and robot in your home in one simple app. It is free, has no ads and needs no account: it talks to your devices directly on your home Wi-Fi.

HOME, YOUR WAY
• Every device in a clear list with a switch. Tap to switch, slide to dim, hold for every control.
• Rooms at the top: tap one to see only what is in it.
• Favourites first, and a status bar that shows what is on, open or not answering.

DRAW YOUR HOME
• Draw your rooms on a floor plan and put each device where it really is.
• Several floors, and several homes, such as a holiday house or an office.

SCENES AND AUTOMATIONS
• "Good night" turns off the lights and locks the door with one tap.
• Run scenes at a time, at sunrise or sunset, or when something happens: motion, a door, rain, the temperature.
• Turn a device off in 30 minutes with a timer.

WORKS WITH WHAT YOU HAVE
Matter (no hub needed), Philips Hue, Shelly, Tuya and Smart Life, Home Assistant, ESPHome, Tasmota, WLED, MQTT, iRobot Roomba, Samsung, LG, Sony and Roku TVs, Chromecast, Tapo, Reolink, Hikvision, Imou and other cameras, MikroTik routers, and anything on your network that a scan finds.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your homes are saved on your own device.
• An optional free account keeps them in step on every phone and computer and lets you control them from anywhere. You can delete it at any time in the app.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Available in English and Bulgarian.
